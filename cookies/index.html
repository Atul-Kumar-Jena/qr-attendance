What we keep on your device.

We use a handful of small browser-side items so Attendly works smoothly. No ads, no trackers, no resold data.

Effective: May 2026 · This page covers only your local browser storage. For the service agreement, see Terms & Conditions.

Essential — always on
🔑
always on
Sign-in token

When you sign in with Google, we save a tiny token so you stay signed in next visit. Without this, you'd have to log in on every page.

🎨
always on
Theme & display

Your light / dark / auto choice, your accent colour, and any layout tweaks you make. Just so the site looks the way you like it.

Helpful — improve your experience
🧭
Guided tour memory

The first time you open a new section, a quick walkthrough appears. We remember you've seen it so it doesn't reappear.

🎯
Onboarding progress

If you're a new student or institution, we save your step in the join flow — so refreshing the page doesn't start it over.

Security — fraud prevention
🛡️
Device fingerprint

A small random hash of your browser. Helps Attendly tell your scans apart from impostors. Never sent to anyone outside Attendly.

✗ What we never use

  • ●Third-party advertising cookies
  • ●Cross-site tracking pixels
  • ●Behavioural profiling for marketers
  • ●Selling or sharing data with brokers
  • ●Fingerprinting for ad networks

In your control

You can clear everything we've saved on your device in one click. Your sign-in, theme, tour state and device fingerprint will reset.
