What cookies we use.

Attendly is privacy-first. We only store what is required to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and run guided tours — no third-party ad trackers, no fingerprinting, no analytics resold to anyone.

attendly-theme-mode
Necessary

Remembers your light/dark/auto theme preference so the site loads in the right mode.

Lifetime: Persistent (until you change it)
attendly_site_config
Necessary

Caches admin-edited site configuration (pricing labels, stats) so the site renders instantly while Firestore loads.

Lifetime: Persistent
atd_cookies
Necessary

Stores your cookie consent choice so we don't ask again.

Lifetime: 12 months
atd_terms
Necessary

Records that you accepted the Terms of Service.

Lifetime: Persistent
atd_tour_*
Functional

Tracks which guided tours (driver.js) you have already seen so they don't restart.

Lifetime: Persistent
atd_onboarding_*
Functional

Remembers if you completed the new-user onboarding flow (Student / Institution).

Lifetime: Persistent
firebase:authUser:*
Necessary

Keeps you signed in across visits (issued by Firebase Authentication).

Lifetime: Until sign-out
